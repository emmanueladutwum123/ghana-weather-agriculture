**Request for district crop production data for academic research**

I am a final-year student in mathematics and economics at Soka University of America in California. For my senior capstone in economics, supervised by Professor Diya Mazumder, I am studying how rainfall and temperature affect crop output in Ghana. I have used the national tables in *Agriculture in Ghana: Facts and Figures* (the 2018 to 2021 editions), and I am writing to ask whether SRID would share the district-level data behind them for non-commercial research.

I would like, for each district and year, the cropped area, production and yield of maize, rice (paddy), millet, sorghum, cassava and yam, from the Multi-Round Annual Crop and Livestock Survey and the Ghana Agricultural Production Survey. I would like as long a period as is available, ideally from 2000 to the most recent year. If SRID also holds the monthly rural and urban market prices for these crops by district, those would be useful too.

The published reports give national totals and the ten highest-yielding districts for each crop. A complete district panel would let me compare districts that had a dry or wet season with those that did not, which national data cannot do. I would match the data to monthly rainfall and temperature for each district from freely available satellite and reanalysis sources.

I will use the data only for this academic project. I will not share the raw data with anyone else, and I will publish only aggregated results and figures, with SRID credited as the source. I am glad to sign a data-use agreement if one is required, and I will send SRID a copy of the finished paper. If there is a fee, please let me know the amount. I would also be grateful to know whether editions of *Facts and Figures* after 2021 have been published, since I could not find them on the Ministry's website.

An electronic format such as Excel or CSV would be most useful, but any format is welcome.

Thank you for considering this request.
