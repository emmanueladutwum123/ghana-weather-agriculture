I am a final-year student in mathematics and economics at Soka University of America in California. I am writing to ask whether COCOBOD would share two data series for this non-commercial research.

1. Weekly graded and sealed cocoa purchases, national totals, for as many crop seasons as are available, ideally from the 2000/01 season to the current season. If possible, I would also like the same series by region or district, and the split between the main crop and the light crop.
